2. Embrace experimentation

This unit particularly helped me to embrace experimentation—and failure—in my own work. Before this unit, I had very little interaction with code or knowledge on adapting webpages. Compared to my classmates, I felt behind. I had to spend time experimenting with html, mainly through failing and finding ways to fix the issue. There were times were I gave up and had to ask for help, specifically with the multi-page webtext assignment. However, experimenting and failing was all part of the learning process. /p>

3.Engage with others in a workshop environment

Workshopping our projects has been been my favorite method of engaging in this class. I found myself most inspired to experiment with html after seeing the cool ways my classmates were manipulating their own work. Engaging with others also made me consider the theories, methods, and practices that went into my own work. From there, I was able to clearly articulate my process.

4. Practice revision within and across media forms

There were many assignments that I had to revise simply based off of technical errors. For the new media assignment, I spent over an hour trying to get my GitHub page to not say "404 Error". Finally, I brought my issue to class Monday and had a classmate help me to revise my project and correct the error.

5. Learn to present your work

While I only formally presented my work to the entire class once, I had to learn how to present my failures and shortcomings repeatedly to my classmates. This incuded discussing what went wrong, where it went wrong, and asking for proposed solutions on how to fix issues.
